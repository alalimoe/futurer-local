## Delivery & shipping

Nootropix fulfills orders from Dubai, UAE via **Quiqup**. We use two distinct delivery services:

### Dubai — 4-hour express

- **Service:** Quiqup 4-hour express (on-demand courier)
- **How it works:** A driver is dispatched for your order, picks up a single parcel, and delivers door-to-door
- **Guarantee:** Delivery within **4 hours** of dispatch (typically under 2 hours)
- **Cutoff:** Orders placed Mon–Sat before 6:00 PM Dubai time qualify for same-day dispatch

### Rest of UAE — next-day

- **Service:** Quiqup next-day daily batch
- **How it works:** Orders are collected once per day, processed, and delivered the next business day
- **Coverage:** Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, RAK, Fujairah, and other emirates

### Saudi Arabia & international

- **Service:** Quiqup international (collected in daily batch, exported via global shipping)
- **KSA timing:** **3–5 business days** on average after dispatch from Dubai
- **Other countries:** Timing depends on customs clearance and destination; contact support for estimates

### Shipping costs

| Destination | Cost |
|---|---|
| **UAE (all orders)** | **Free** — no minimum |
| **International orders ≥ 300 AED** | **Free shipping** |
| **International orders < 300 AED** | **85 AED** flat rate worldwide |

Duties, taxes, and import fees may apply for international orders and are the responsibility of the recipient unless stated otherwise at checkout.

## Delivery failure policy

| Scenario | Handling |
|---|---|
| Our fault (mispack, label error, courier error on our instructions) | **Full refund** |
| Local delivery failure (UAE RTO after re-attempts) | **Reship or full refund** (customer choice) |
| International customs seizure/hold | **No refund** — buyer assumes import risk |
| Courtesy store credit (intl customs, first occurrence per customer) | Discretionary one-time store credit — **not guaranteed**, not a standing right |
| Customer-caused (wrong address entered, refused delivery) | No automatic refund |
