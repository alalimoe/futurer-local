**Return & Refund Policy**

Nootropix M.E. Para Pharmaceutical Products Trading ("Nootropix") wants you to be satisfied with your purchase. This policy explains how returns and refunds work.

**Return window**

You may return eligible items within **30 days** of the delivery date shown on your tracking confirmation.

**Eligibility**

- Items must be **unopened**, **sealed**, and in **resalable condition**
- Opened, used, or tampered products cannot be returned
- Proof of purchase (order number or email) is required

**How to start a return**

1. Email **support@nootropix.shop** with your order number and the item(s) you wish to return
2. We will provide return instructions and a return label or pickup arrangement where applicable
3. Pack items securely in original packaging when possible

**Return shipping**

Return shipping is **free**. Nootropix covers the cost of returning eligible items.

**Refunds**

- Refunds are processed after we **receive and inspect** the returned item(s)
- Please allow **7–14 business days** from receipt of your return for the refund to be issued
- Refunds are returned to your **original payment method**
- Shipping charges are non-refundable except where required by law or where the return is due to our error

**Orders that do not arrive**

If your order does not arrive and is confirmed lost in transit or returned to us undeliverable, you are entitled to a **full refund**. Refunds for non-delivery are issued after carrier status is verified; this may take additional time beyond standard return processing.

**Exchanges**

We do not process direct exchanges. To exchange an item, return the eligible product per this policy and place a new order.
