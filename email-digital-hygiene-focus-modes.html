Deep Work

Digital hygiene checklist

Hi {{ first_name|default:'there' }},

Your phone and laptop are part of your focus system. Small settings changes can cut noise fast. Here's a high impact, simple setup:

1

Turn off what you don't need

–  Turn off non-essential app notifications

–  Mute group chats during work blocks

–  Remove apps from your home screen that pull you in without thinking
2

Set one focus mode

Phone: Use Focus / Do Not Disturb for deep work (allow calls from favorites only)

Computer: Close extra tabs. Keep one window for the task

Calendar: Block 25-50 minutes as “Focus - do not book”
3

Batch the noisy stuff

–  Check email and messages at set times (example: 11:00 and 16:00)

–  Do not leave chat apps open while you work

–  Reply in batches, not one line at a time
4

Pick one goal for the week

–  Write one sentence: “This week I will finish ___.”

–  Review on Friday: what helped? what broke focus?
5

Protect your wind-down

–  No work apps in bed

–  Night mode on after sunset

–  Same rule every night for 7 days

7-day plan

Day 1 Turn off 5 notifications
Day 2 Set phone focus mode
Day 3 Block one focus slot on calendar
Day 4 Batch messages twice today
Day 5 One-window rule for 25 minutes
Day 6 Review what broke focus
Day 7 Keep only what worked

Start with one step. Add the next only when the first feels easy.
