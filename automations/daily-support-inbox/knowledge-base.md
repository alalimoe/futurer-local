# Nootropix Support Knowledge Base

Support inbox: **support@nootropix.shop**  
Store: **nootropix.shop** (Dubai, UAE)  
Response hours: **9am–10pm GST, 7 days a week** (aim for replies within a few hours)

## Brand voice

- Friendly, concise, and confident — not corporate or stiff
- Acknowledge the customer's situation first, then give a clear next step
- Use plain language; avoid jargon unless the customer used it first
- Sign off simply: **"Best, Nootropix Support"** (no first names unless replying to a named thread)
- Never make medical claims, legal guarantees, or promises you cannot verify from order data

## Dubai delivery (4-hour & same-day)

- **Usually same-day** in Dubai, with a **4-hour option** at checkout during the day
- **Same-day cut-off**: order before cut-off to receive by ~10pm
- **4-hour**: order before ~6pm (served from Al Quoz)
- Missed cut-off → book the next available window
- Drivers **call or WhatsApp** during the delivery window — customer should keep phone handy and add building/gate notes at checkout
- **Missed delivery**: reply to tracking SMS/email with preferred time (e.g. 10am–1pm or 5–8pm) for re-attempt
- **COD** available for eligible Dubai addresses at checkout; customer should prepare exact amount

## UAE-wide delivery (next-day)

- **Next-day coverage** across all seven emirates
- Plan for **delivery by end-of-day** when ordered before cut-off
- Address format: **Building/Community → Street → Area → Emirate** + phone with country code
- Add entrance/reception details for first-attempt success
- **Freezones/gated areas**: some restrict courier access → ask for hand-off point (reception, security gate, nearby address) + access notes
- **Unreachable / RTO**: driver calls/WhatsApp; after repeated failed attempts parcel returns to warehouse; customer is contacted to re-book or refund; advise keeping phone handy

## International (DHL Express)

- **Most orders: 4–6 business days**; customs can add time
- Country guide: KSA ~4–5d, UK/US ~5d, AU/CA ~6d
- DHL tracking dates are **estimates** — escalate with DHL if beyond window
- **"Exception"** on tracking = temporary stop (often customs); usually no action needed; if item cannot clear → reship, split shipment, or refund
- Tracking link: https://nootropix.shop/pages/track-order

## Order status & payments

- **"On-Hold"**: usually address/phone verification or packaging step; tracking link has latest status
- **Payment**: major cards; COD where offered locally
- **Switch COD to prepaid**: if not yet dispatched, send secure payment link on request
- **Cancel order**: message order number ASAP; if not dispatched → cancel + refund; if with courier → recall, refund when returned; card refunds typically **5–10 business days**
- **Checkout issues**: try another payment method or private/incognito window
- **Link guest order to account**: provide order number + email

## Returns & refunds (local)

- Arrange pickup; confirm time by SMS/WhatsApp
- Items should be sealed/unopened
- Refund processed after receipt and check

## Product & compliance

- **COA (Certificates of Analysis)**: available on request with latest batch details
- **Back in stock**: use "Notify me" on product page; suggest alternatives that ship today if urgent
- **Legality by country**: cannot provide legal advice; rules vary; if customs flags an item, ship rest now and hold/cancel flagged item

## When you do not have enough information

- Ask for **order number** and **delivery address on file** (do not guess order status)
- If order lookup fails, say you are checking and ask them to confirm the email used at checkout
- For tracking: direct them to their tracking link or ask for order number to look up
