One focus block before your inbox

Hi {{ first_name|default:'there' }},

Most mornings start in reactive mode. You open your laptop, check messages, and the day takes over before you do one real thing.

Here is a simple fix: one focus block before your inbox. Twenty-five minutes. One tab. One task.

One tab. One task. Twenty-five minutes.

No inbox. No chat. No “quick checks.” Just one clear job until the timer ends.

1

Pick one task

–  Choose one task you can finish or move forward in 25 minutes

–  Write it in one line: “In this block I will ___.”

–  If it feels too big, cut it smaller until it fits 25 minutes
2

Set up one tab

–  Close extra tabs and apps

–  Keep one window open for the task

–  Put your phone in another room, or turn on Focus / Do Not Disturb
3

Start the timer

–  Set a timer for 25 minutes

–  Work only on that one task until it rings

–  If your mind drifts, write the distraction on paper and return
4

Stop on time

–  When the timer ends, stop (even mid-sentence)

–  Take a 2-minute break: stand up, water, breathe

–  Then check messages once, in one batch
5

Repeat tomorrow

–  Same rule. Same order: focus block first, inbox second

–  After 3 days, notice what breaks the block (notifications, tabs, “quick” replies)

Why this works

One tab cuts switching cost.

One task gives your brain a clear target.

Twenty-five minutes is long enough to start, short enough to protect.

You are not trying to be perfect all day. You are trying to win the first block.

4-day starter plan

Thu One 25-min block before inbox
Fri Same block, phone in another room
Sat Optional: one block for personal admin
Sun Review: what broke focus? Remove one distraction

Start with one block today. Do not add more rules until this feels easy.

PS: If you only do one thing today, do the 25-minute block before you open email.
