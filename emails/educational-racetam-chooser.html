“Which racetam should I choose?” is the wrong first question.

A better one:

What kind of workday are you trying to get through?

The three below sit in the same family, but they make sense in very different contexts.
01

The Demanding Day

Phenylpiracetam Hydrazide

Think: deadlines, long task lists, high-output work.

This is the more drive-oriented option of the three.

It tends to make the most sense when the problem isn’t what to do. It’s getting enough momentum behind a demanding day.

A better fit for:

→ Heavy workloads

→ Long focus blocks

→ Days where mental drive matters

Less suited to: the days when you already feel overstimulated.

Explore Phenylpiracetam →
02

The Creative Day

Aniracetam

Think: writing, design, strategy, brainstorming.

Aniracetam sits on the calmer side of the racetam family.

Rather than choosing it for brute-force output, people usually look at it when they want to stay mentally engaged without making the day feel more intense.

A better fit for:

→ Creative work

→ Flexible thinking

→ Calm, productive sessions

Less suited to: days where your main problem is lack of drive.

Explore Aniracetam →
03

The Quieter Day

Fasoracetam

Think: reading, studying, steady desk work.

Fasoracetam is the lower-key option here.

It’s generally the one to look at when you want something more subtle than a “push harder” type of racetam.

A better fit for:

→ Steady cognitive work

→ Lower-intensity days

→ A quieter racetam profile

Less suited to: days where you’re looking for obvious stimulation.

Explore Fasoracetam →

The 10-second chooser

Need drive?
