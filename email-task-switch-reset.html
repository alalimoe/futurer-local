5-Minute Protocol

The task-switch reset

You closed the report tab. Slack is already asking for something else. Your brain is still halfway in the last task.

{{ first_name|default:'Hey' }}, that lag is not laziness. Task switching has a cost — your attention keeps one foot in what you just left. Use this 5-minute bridge every time you switch: email to report, Slack to study, Project A to Project B.

Why this, not Pomodoro alone

Pomodoro helps you focus inside a block. Digital hygiene reduces noise. Neither gives you a clean bridge between tasks. This reset is a transition ritual — not just “focus harder.”

The 5-minute reset

1

Park the old task

One line only: “Stopped here / next step is…” Write it. Do not carry it in your head.
 
2

Clear the switch cost

Close the tabs. Stand up. Three slow breaths. Let the last task go before the next one starts.
 
3

Open the new task with one sentence

“Now I will…” One outcome. Not a to-do list.
 
4

Start a 25-minute timer. Phone away.

One block. One thread.

Practice on every switch for 7 days — not only after meetings, not only when you are already behind. The goal is not to focus harder. It is to transition clean so the next task gets your full attention.
